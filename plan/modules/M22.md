# M22 — Pilot protocol readiness

## Responsibility

Prepare the staged internal pilot per protocol — cohort/task matrix, metrics/thresholds, participant rights and support, issue/decision log — and record STOP/REBUILD/RESTRICT/MODIFY/LIMITED/PHASED/PROCEED.

## Acceptance criteria

- WHILE the Stage 12A gate has not passed or restricted, THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT start any real employee pilot.
