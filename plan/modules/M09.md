# M09 — Eligibility router & canonical fallback

## Responsibility

Hard eligibility (policy/region/features/qualification/health) before minimum-sufficient cost/latency selection; canonical auth/quota/model/region/schema/qualification/resource/no-route/user-action failure states with terminal human-review/no-AI paths; every rejected candidate reason auditable.

## Acceptance criteria

- WHEN candidates are filtered, THE SYSTEM SHALL record every rejected candidate's reason auditably, and cost SHALL NOT make an ineligible route eligible.
- WHEN no eligible route exists, THE SYSTEM SHALL resolve to the canonical no-route/human-review/no-AI terminal — never a silent material route change.
- WHEN AI is unavailable, THE SYSTEM SHALL never convert that into employee failure (journey tests).

## Verification (required evidence)

Routing, chaos, region, cost and employee-journey tests (HND-007); failure-injection tests (REM-010).
