# M05 — Provider-scoped credential resolution

## Responsibility

Replace the Anthropic-specific nullable override with an explicit provider/tenant credential result carrying configured/not-found/unavailable/forbidden and policy state; fail safely per policy.

## Acceptance criteria

- WHEN the tenant credential service is unavailable, THE SYSTEM SHALL fail per policy and SHALL NOT silently switch to a platform key, account, provider or data route.
- WHEN credentials rotate, THE SYSTEM SHALL pass rotation, outage, tenant and identity tests without cross-tenant leakage.

## Verification (required evidence)

Rotation, outage, tenant and identity tests (HND-004).
