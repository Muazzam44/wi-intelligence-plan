<non_negotiable_rules>
- No pilot begins until the DPIA, UK legal review, applicable Pakistan/Lahore legal review, worker consultation, monitoring notice/policy, security architecture and participant-protection terms are approved for the actual pilot scope.
- No personal devices, covert mode, audio, webcam, continuous full-motion recording, broad network payload capture or unrestricted clipboard capture in the pilot.
- Zero known password, authentication-code, private-key or high-severity secret retention is a blocking requirement.
- No unauthorised raw-evidence access, cross-tenant access or unapproved cross-border transfer is acceptable.
- Pilot outputs cannot solely or primarily determine dismissal, discipline, promotion, pay, probation or equivalent adverse employment action.
- Employees must be able to view material evidence, correct context, dispute findings, request meaningful human review and see material raw-evidence access.
- Missing telemetry, unsupported tools, excluded periods and degraded services cannot be interpreted as inactivity or failure.
- Clicks, keystrokes, hours, code volume, prompt count, token use and time online are not productivity, quality or capability scores.
- Every AI/evaluator conclusion must include evidence, confidence, completeness, version, limitations and alternative explanations.
- High-impact findings and capability/readiness changes require qualified human review.
- Accessibility accommodations and alternate valid work methods cannot create lower conclusions.
- Training completion alone cannot increase capability; reviewed applied evidence is required.
- Skill Forge cannot deploy unreviewed or unsigned packages; canary and rollback must remain operational throughout the pilot.
- DPO, CISO and executive sponsor each retain authority to pause affected capture, a feature, a package, a cohort or the entire pilot.
- A partial or inconclusive result must be reported honestly; no launch recommendation may hide unresolved critical risk.
</non_negotiable_rules>
