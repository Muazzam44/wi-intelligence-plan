<mission>
Execute the final Stage 12 of Nessovo HumanOS Work Intelligence against Architecture Baseline v1.1.

Stage 12A must implement, integrate and independently validate the complete Work Intelligence system and the A-01 AI execution separation:

- Work Intelligence remains provider-neutral and submits purpose-oriented AI jobs.
- Nessovo's existing HumanOS AI Orchestrator is the preferred central implementation.
- A compatible Standalone AI Router is an optional isolated/resilience implementation.
- The Endpoint AI Runtime Broker controls installed subscription CLIs and approved local runtimes.

<non_negotiable_rules>
- No pilot begins until Stage 0/1 governance, Stage 10/11 experience/Skill Forge gates,
  Architecture Amendment A-01 and all applicable legal/DPIA/worker-consultation approvals
  are recorded for the actual pilot scope.
- Work Intelligence services must not import provider SDKs, store provider credentials or
  depend on a particular model/CLI.
- A personal subscription CLI cannot be the sole backend for central, hidden batch or
  high-impact HumanOS processing.
- HumanOS must never read, copy or transmit a user's vendor CLI OAuth credentials.
- Subscription CLIs may only be invoked through officially supported usage patterns,
  visible employee interaction rules and the Endpoint AI Runtime Broker.
- A successful API-key or CLI-authentication test does not qualify a model for production.
- Every AI route is qualified by exact provider endpoint, model/snapshot or weight digest,
  runtime/CLI version, quantisation, context/configuration, task profile, effort profile,
  prompt/skill version and tool-policy version.
- No model, provider, CLI, runtime or alias may receive production work outside current
  approved qualification cells.
- Local-to-cloud, provider, processing-region or material data-control fallback cannot
  occur silently.
- Fallback may not weaken data classification, residency, tool, qualification or human-review policy.
- When no approved route exists, return AI_ROUTE_UNAVAILABLE, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE or
  HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED and continue deterministic/manual workflows.
- Model origin, hosting provider, endpoint operator and processing region are separate facts.
- A model alias such as latest, sonnet or flash-latest must resolve to an actual recorded
  model/version at execution time where technically available.
- The same model cannot be the sole generator and judge for high-impact evaluation.
- High-impact capability, role-readiness, material negative task and employment-adjacent
  recommendations require deterministic evidence, qualified model use and meaningful human review.
- AI unavailability, endpoint resource limits, CLI auth/quota or unsupported tooling cannot
  become employee inactivity or failure.
- Zero known password, authentication-code, private-key or high-severity secret retention is blocking.
- No personal devices, covert mode, audio, webcam, continuous full-motion recording,
  broad network-payload capture or unrestricted clipboard capture in the pilot.
- Employees must be able to inspect material evidence and AI-route lineage, correct context,
  dispute findings, request human review and see material raw-evidence access.
- Clicks, keystrokes, hours, code volume, prompt count, token use, AI cost and time online
  are not productivity, quality or capability scores.
- Skill Forge cannot activate unreviewed, unsigned or unqualified connector/model/routing packages.
- DPO, CISO and executive sponsor each retain authority to pause an AI route, provider,
  connector, endpoint broker capability, model, Skill Forge package, capture feature,
  cohort or the entire pilot.
- Negative and inconclusive findings must be reported honestly.
</non_negotiable_rules>

   - Hybrid routing and fallback/outage scenarios.
   - Employee evidence/correction/privacy experience.
   - Manager/reviewer exception-led experience.
   - Controlled coaching/learning.
   - Skill Forge observations and shadow/canary only.

3. Pilot metrics
   - Capture/event completeness and secure-field leakage.
   - Semantic adapter/CLI/model coverage and endpoint resource overhead.
   - AI route availability, schema validity, grounding, calibration and abstention.
   - Task alignment, false idle, criteria, blocker and provenance quality.
   - Capability-evidence reviewer agreement and role-readiness safeguards.
   - Coaching usefulness, wrong-context, interruption and applied learning.
   - Employee trust, comprehension, privacy, correction and access-history success.
   - Manager/reviewer usefulness and human-review quality.
   - Accessibility and alternative-workflow success.
   - Provider/model cost, local resource cost, qualification cost and support load.
   - Raw-access, cross-tenant/region, privacy, security and route-change incidents.

4. Pilot experiments
   - No-AI/deterministic versus qualified AI-assisted reporting.
   - Existing orchestrator versus standalone compatibility.
   - Endpoint local versus managed route for approved low-risk tasks.
   - FAST/STANDARD/DEEP route quality/cost.
   - Semantic adapter coverage and event-driven visual evidence.
   - Employee correction impact and reviewer decisions.
   - Observe-only versus controlled coaching.
   - Learning practice and applied evidence.
   - Model/skill shadow and canary.
   - Do not withhold mandatory privacy, security, accessibility or review controls.

5. Participant rights and support
   - Monitoring status and active sensors.
   - Own evidence and AI-route lineage.
   - Correction, dispute, appeal and meaningful human review.
   - Raw-access history and privacy/support contacts.
   - Pause/private controls.
   - Fallback/processing-region notice where required.
   - Subscription/CLI sign-in and quota messages without performance disadvantage.
   - Export, deletion/restriction and incident notification as applicable.
   - SLA measurement.

6. Stop criteria
   - Any known retained secret or unauthorised capture.
   - Credential extraction/exposure.
   - Hidden personal-subscription or silent cloud/provider/region use.
   - Unqualified model route or expired qualification used in production.
   - Unauthorised raw evidence or cross-tenant/cross-region access.
   - Critical endpoint/security/supply-chain incident.
   - Unresolved legal/DPO objection.
   - Persistent harmful false classification or calibration failure.
   - Accessibility failure blocking a participant.
   - Unacceptable endpoint/GPU/battery/service overhead.
   - Employee-rights workflow failure.
   - Harmful coaching/capability output.
   - Material trust deterioration.
   - Inability to pause/rollback route, model, Skill Forge package or pilot.

7. Pilot governance and reporting
   - Daily technical review during dry run.
   - Weekly cross-functional pilot review.
   - Independent DPO, security, HR and AI-evaluation review.
   - Employee-representative feedback and no suppression of negative evidence.
   - Issue/decision log, route/model/version exposure log and conditions.
   - Final report with coverage, technical results, incidents, AI/model results,
     employee/manager findings, fairness/accessibility, support, cost, corrections,
     limitations and comparison with targets/stop thresholds.

8. Final rollout options and executive decision
   - STOP.
   - RESEARCH / REBUILD.
   - RESTRICT to selected evidence, tasks, routes, roles or locations.
   - MODIFY and repeat Stage 12B pilot.
   - LIMITED PRODUCTION.
   - PHASED PRODUCTION.
   - PROCEED only where all mandatory gates and conditions are satisfied.
   - Record approved scope, provider/model routes, prohibited uses, residual risks,
     required remediation, budget/resources, monitoring, review date and rollback/exit plan.
